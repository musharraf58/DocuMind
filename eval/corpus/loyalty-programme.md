# Larkspur Home: Lantern Loyalty Programme

Larkspur Home is a fictional company invented for the DocuMind retrieval
evaluation. Every name, number and rule in this document is made up.

## Joining

Lantern is free. Every customer with a Larkspur account can join from the
account settings page; you need to be at least 18 years old. Membership is
personal and points cannot be transferred to another account.

## Earning points

You earn one point for every euro spent in the web shop or in our showrooms,
excluding shipping costs and gift card purchases. Points appear in your
account once the 30-day return window of the order has closed, so that
returned products do not earn points.

You also earn points for things other than buying:

- 50 points for writing a product review with a photo;
- 100 points when a friend you invited places their first order;
- 25 points on your birthday, if you have added it to your profile.

## Tiers

Members move up a tier based on points earned in the last twelve months:

- Ember: 0 to 499 points;
- Flame: 500 to 1,499 points, which adds free express delivery;
- Blaze: 1,500 points or more, which adds free two-person delivery and early
  access to the winter sale.

Tiers are checked on the first day of every month.

## Spending points

Every 100 points are worth 5 euros off a future order. You can redeem at most
half of an order's value with points. Points cannot be exchanged for cash and
cannot be used to buy gift cards.

## Expiry

Points expire 24 months after they were earned. We send a reminder email 30
days before any points expire. Closing your account deletes your points
immediately; they cannot be restored if you open a new account later.

## Changes to the programme

We may change the programme with 60 days' notice by email. Points you have
already earned keep their value for at least those 60 days.
