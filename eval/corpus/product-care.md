# Larkspur Home: Caring for Your Products

Larkspur Home is a fictional company invented for the DocuMind retrieval
evaluation. Every name, number and rule in this document is made up.

## Lamps and shades

Dust fabric shades once a week with a soft, dry brush or a lint roller. Never
use water or a damp cloth on the Willow shade: the linen shrinks and the
seams can pull away from the frame. Glass shades can go in the dishwasher on a
cool programme, at most 40 degrees, on the top rack.

Larkspur lamps use integrated LEDs that are designed to last about 25,000
hours, roughly 15 years of normal evening use. There is no bulb to replace.
If an LED starts flickering, check first that the dimmer on your wall switch
is compatible with LED lighting; older dimmers cause most flicker complaints.

## Batteries

Rechargeable products such as the Pebble speaker and the Glow table lamp last
longest when the battery is kept between 20 and 80 percent. If you store a
product for more than a month, charge it to about half first and top it up
every three months. Do not leave products charging in direct sunlight or on a
radiator: heat is what wears a battery out fastest.

Always use the supplied cable or the LH-0417 adapter. Cheap chargers with the
wrong voltage are the most common cause of battery faults we see.

## Wooden furniture

Wipe wooden surfaces with a slightly damp cloth and dry them straight away.
Once a year, treat oiled oak (such as the Cedar sideboard) with a thin layer
of hardwax oil. Rings left by hot cups can often be removed by placing a
cotton cloth over the mark and running a warm, dry iron over it for a few
seconds.

## Textiles

Cushion covers and throws can be machine washed at 30 degrees, inside out,
and should be dried flat. The wool throw from the Fjell collection must be
hand washed in cold water with wool detergent; a machine wash will felt it.

## Diffusers

Clean the Aroma diffuser after every cartridge by wiping the nozzle with a
cotton bud dipped in white vinegar. Use only Larkspur cartridges: other oils
can clog the nozzle and are not covered by the warranty.

## Moving house

Pack lamps in their original boxes if you still have them. Remove glass shades
and wrap them separately. Floor lamps over 1.6 metres can be taken apart at
the middle joint; turn the upper half anticlockwise to release it.
