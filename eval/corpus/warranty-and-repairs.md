# Larkspur Home: Warranty and Repairs

Larkspur Home is a fictional company invented for the DocuMind retrieval
evaluation. Every name, number and rule in this document is made up.

## What the warranty covers

Every Larkspur product comes with a two-year manufacturer's warranty from the
date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship:
a lamp that stops switching on, a motor that grinds, a seam that splits under
normal use. It does not cover damage from drops, water, power surges, or
repairs carried out by anyone other than an authorised Larkspur repair centre.

Rechargeable batteries are covered for one year, not two, because every
battery slowly loses capacity. A battery is considered faulty if it holds less
than 70 percent of its original charge within that first year.

The Larkspur Halo ceiling lamp has an extended five-year warranty on its LED
panel. The driver electronics inside the Halo are covered for the standard two
years.

## Making a warranty claim

Open a claim from the order page in your account and describe the fault.
Our support team usually answers within one working day. For most faults we
ask for a short video showing the problem; this lets us decide whether a
repair, a replacement part or a full replacement is the right fix.

You need the order number or a receipt. If the product was a gift, the gift
receipt is enough: the warranty transfers to whoever owns the product.

## Repair or replacement

We repair first and replace second. If the repair centre cannot fix a product
within 21 days, or the same fault comes back after a repair, we replace the
product with a new one of the same model or, when that model is no longer
sold, its current successor.

Replacement products carry the remainder of the original warranty or six
months, whichever is longer.

## Spare parts

We keep spare parts for every product for seven years after we stop selling
it. Common parts can be ordered directly in the web shop:

- part LH-2210, the fabric shade for the Willow floor lamp;
- part LH-3305, the replacement remote control for the Halo;
- part ZX-9, the battery clip for the Pebble portable speaker;
- part LH-0417, the USB-C power adapter (30 W) shared by most table lamps.

Fitting a part from this list yourself does not void the warranty, provided
you follow the instructions in the manual.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

After the warranty ends we still repair products at the Eindhoven repair
centre. Diagnosis costs a flat 25 euros, which is deducted from the repair
price if you go ahead. We send a quote before starting any paid work and never
repair without your written approval.

## Recycling old products

When a product cannot be repaired economically we take it back for free and
recycle it. You receive a voucher worth 10 percent of the original price
towards a new Larkspur product, valid for 90 days.
