# Larkspur Home: Shipping and Delivery

Larkspur Home is a fictional company invented for the DocuMind retrieval
evaluation. Every name, number and rule in this document is made up.

## Where we deliver

We ship to the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Germany and France. We do
not ship to the Canary Islands, the Channel Islands or any address outside
the European Union mainland, and we do not deliver to parcel lockers for items
heavier than 15 kilograms.

## Delivery times

Orders placed before 22:00 on a working day leave our warehouse the same
night. Standard delivery then takes one working day in the Netherlands and
two to four working days to the other countries. During the winter sale,
allow two extra working days.

Large items such as floor lamps over 1.6 metres and the Larkspur Cedar
sideboard are delivered by a two-person crew on a scheduled day. The crew
calls 30 minutes before arrival and carries the item to the room of your
choice, but does not assemble furniture.

## Delivery costs

Shipping is free on orders of 50 euros or more. Below that we charge 3.95
euros in the Netherlands and 6.95 euros to other countries. Two-person
delivery costs a flat 39 euros, regardless of how many large items you order.

Express delivery, before 12:00 the next working day, costs 9.95 euros and is
only available in the Netherlands and Belgium.

## Tracking your parcel

You receive a tracking link by email as soon as the parcel leaves the
warehouse. The tracking code starts with the letters LKS followed by ten
digits. If the tracking page has not updated for three working days, contact
support and we will open an investigation with the courier.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is home the courier tries once more the next working day, then
takes the parcel to the nearest pick-up point. Parcels wait there for seven
days before they are sent back to us. Returned parcels are refunded in full,
minus the original shipping cost.

## Damaged or lost parcels

Check the parcel when it arrives. If the box is visibly damaged, take a photo
before opening it and report it within 48 hours. We send a replacement
straight away without waiting for the courier's investigation. A parcel that
shows as delivered but never reached you must be reported within 14 days.

## Customs

Because we only ship within the European Union, no customs duties or import
VAT apply to any order.
