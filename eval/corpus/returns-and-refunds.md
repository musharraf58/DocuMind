# Larkspur Home: Returns and Refunds Policy

Larkspur Home is a fictional company invented for the DocuMind retrieval
evaluation. Every name, number and rule in this document is made up.

## Who this policy covers

This policy applies to every product bought directly from the Larkspur Home
web shop or from one of our own showrooms. Products bought from a reseller,
a marketplace seller or a second-hand platform must be returned to the place
they were bought; we cannot refund an order we never received payment for.

## The return window

You can return most products within 30 days of the delivery date for any
reason, including simply changing your mind. The 30 days start on the day the
courier marks the parcel as delivered, not on the day you placed the order.
Products bought during the winter sale (1 December to 15 January) can be
returned until 31 January, even when that is more than 30 days after delivery.

Some products cannot be returned once they are opened, for hygiene reasons:
in-ear headphones, the Larkspur Aroma diffuser cartridges and replacement
toothbrush heads. These can still be returned if the seal is unbroken.

Custom-engraved lamps cannot be returned at all, unless they arrive damaged
or the engraving does not match your order.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in to your Larkspur account and open the order.
2. Choose "Return items", pick the products and a reason.
3. Print the prepaid label, or show the QR code at a drop-off point.

You will receive a return number that starts with RMA, for example
RMA-208113. Write it on the outside of the parcel. Parcels without a return
number take up to three extra weeks to process, because our warehouse has to
match them to an order by hand.

Return shipping is free when the product is faulty or we sent the wrong item.
For change-of-mind returns we deduct a flat return shipping fee of 4.95 euros
from the refund.

## Condition of returned products

Products should come back in their original packaging with all accessories,
cables and manuals. If something is missing or the product shows signs of use
beyond what is needed to try it out, we may reduce the refund by up to 40
percent. We will always email photos before applying a reduction.

## When you get your money back

We inspect every return within 5 working days of it arriving at the
warehouse in Tilburg. Once the inspection is passed, the refund is paid within
14 days to the original payment method. Bank transfers can take another
2 to 3 working days to appear on your statement.

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If you paid with a
gift card, the amount is restored to that gift card, which stays valid for
two years from the date of the refund.

## Exchanges

We do not do direct exchanges. Return the product for a refund and place a
new order for the item you want instead; this is the fastest way to get it.

## Price drops

If the price of a product you bought drops within 14 days of your order, we
refund the difference once per order. Contact support with your order number;
price drops during flash sales that last less than 24 hours are excluded.
